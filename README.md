# MCM Continuum Physics Series  
## Physics–MCM Correspondence  
### Emergence of Physical Structures from Monistic Geometry

The article **Physics–MCM Correspondence** forms the starting point of the entire **MCM Continuum Physics Series**.

It demonstrates that physics and the Monistic Continuum Model (MCM) are not competing theories but two complementary descriptive levels of the same reality. Physics describes the world **after** the emergence of space, whereas the MCM describes the world **before** the emergence of space.

Physical quantities such as **mass, energy, spacetime, fields, and particles** are interpreted as projections of the **pre-spatial geometry of the medium**. This establishes a formal bridge between established physics and monistic ontology.
